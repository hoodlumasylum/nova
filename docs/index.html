Nova is a classroom and home helper from Hoodlum Asylum. Not everyone is selected. You can apply again later.

Who can apply

The Nova Unique Development Program is for children aged 6 to 15. If you are a student or a parent applying for a child, there is no payment. Answer the questions honestly.

Teachers and other adults applying for themselves may also apply. If you are 16 or over and you apply for yourself, you must do the course Nova is supplied with. Details are sent if you are selected.

Schools, colleges, universities and other organisations apply for a paid licence. Code will reply with pricing. No prices are shown on this page.

What we look for

We look for children with endless ideas of their own — not focused on just one idea, and not copied or influenced by others.

The most unique voices stand out. Write the ideas in the child’s own words.

Powered by true AI

Nova is built to take a child’s ideas seriously. A child can start with simple prompts — even several messy ideas at once — and Nova helps turn those ideas into real outputs they can look at, follow, and keep improving.

With Nova’s help, a child will be able to work towards:

  • app and software builds they can try and change
  • blueprints for how something could be made
  • diagrams that show how an idea fits together
  • step-by-step plans they can follow
  • a learning path that grows with the idea

That helps the child practise building, and it is a way for unique young minds to make things other people can use. We do not promise a finished product, a grade, or any particular result. Nova helps; the child still does the thinking, and a parent or teacher stays involved.
