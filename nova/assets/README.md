`nova-face.jpg` is Nova's official portrait (an original character, not a real person, no logos).
The app uses it as the base for her avatar. Soft masks, skin-sampled lids and a warm light
(`eye-*.png`, `lid-*.png`, `face-warm-light.png`) are derived from that same picture so blinks
and gaze sit in her face. See `nova/face_layout.py`, `nova/qml/Face.qml` and `tools/make_eye_assets.py`.
